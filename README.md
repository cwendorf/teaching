# [`Craig A. Wendorf`](./README.md)

## Teaching Materials

I am a Professor of Psychology and Assistant Dean of the School of Behavioral and Social Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Stevens Point.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This repository is my unofficial teaching portfolio. It is a collection of documents that are useful for my students across my many courses. It is not a replacement for the materials available on Canvas.

### Teaching Interests

- **Psychology Content Courses:** Courses that focus on psychological principles and their applications (e.g., Personality Psychology, Social Psychology, and Cultural Psychology).
- **Psychology Methodology Courses:** Courses that emphasize scientific reasoning and applying methods to psychological questions (e.g., Research Methods in Psychology and Statistics for Psychologists).

### Available Materials

- **[Course Design](./design/README.md):** Explains the philosophy behind my teaching, the learning outcomes for my courses, and recommended learning strategies.
- **[Course Assessment](./assessment/README.md):** Describes grading, student self-assessment, and embedded assessment.
- **[Student Mentoring](./mentoring/README.md):** Describes opportunities to contribute to independent research projects at different stages, from study construction through analysis and presentation.
