# [`Craig A. Wendorf`](./README.md)

## Teaching Materials

I am a Professor of Psychology and Assistant Dean of the School of Behavioral and Social Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Stevens Point.

### Teaching Interests

- **Psychology Content Courses:** Courses that focus on psychological principles and their applications (e.g., Personality Psychology, Social Psychology, and Psychology and Culture).
- **Psychology Methodology Courses:** Courses that emphasize scientific reasoning and applying methods to psychological questions (e.g., Research Methods in Psychology and Statistics for Psychologists).

### Available Materials

- **[Teaching Approach](./approach/README.md):** Explains the ideas behind my teaching and offer strategies for engaging with course materials.
- **[Course Design](./courses/README.md):** Describes what students are expected to learn, how course activities support those outcomes, and how work is evaluated.
- **[Student Mentoring](./mentoring/README.md):** Describes opportunities to contribute to research projects at different stages, from study construction through analysis and presentation.
