## Self-Assessment for Cultural Psychology

### Overview

The American Psychological Association (APA) recommends that instructors identify learning outcomes for each course and assess the extent to which students meet them. Your performance on exams and activities helps me assess your progress toward these outcomes. I am also interested in how course readings, lectures, and activities contribute to your learning.

This survey asks you to rate your ability to meet each outcome before and after the course and to reflect on how course readings, lectures, and activities have supported your learning. Your perceived ability may differ across outcomes.
