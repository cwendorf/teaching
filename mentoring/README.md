## Student Mentoring

### Interests and Topics

My professional interests help frame my mentoring interests, but work with students tends to stretch more broadly into other areas of psychology. Often, we follow what interests us at the time!

- Social and Cultural Psychology: Notions of justice and fairness, influence of social and political values, and interpretation of sarcasm and rumors.
- Applied Statistics and Measurement: Measurement of stress, resilience, life satisfaction, and intercultural preparedness.
- Scholarship of Teaching and Learning: Understanding students' classroom expectations, evaluations of instructors,  academic excuses, and interests in psychological careers.

### Independent Study

Like most of the Psychology faculty, I regularly involve students in my research. Students can become involved in any phase of a research project, from writing a research proposal and designing the study to analyzing and presenting the findings. Although students who want to be part of an entire project become involved on a three-semester basis, many students are involved for only a portion of a project.
