## Student Mentoring

### Independent Study

Like most of the Psychology faculty, I regularly involve students in my research. Students can become involved in any phase of a research project, from writing a research proposal and designing the study to analyzing and presenting the findings. Although students who want to be part of an entire project become involved on a three-semester basis, many students are involved for only a portion of a project.
